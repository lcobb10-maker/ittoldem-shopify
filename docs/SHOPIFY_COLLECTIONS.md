# ITT TOLD’EM Shopify Collection Manifest

The storefront code recognizes these exact handles. Do not change a handle after linking it.

## Customer edits

| Title | Handle | Type | Conditions | Default sort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The House Edit | `house-edit` | Manual | Select 12–18 finished, photographed house signatures | Manual |
| Hoodie Season | `hoodie-season` | Automated | Product tag equals `ready_to_sell` AND `edit_hoodie-season` | Manual |
| Fan Gear — Le Stade | `fan-gear` | Automated | Product tag equals `ready_to_sell` AND `edit_fan-gear` | Manual |
| La Classe — Polished Looks | `la-classe` | Automated | Product tag equals `ready_to_sell` AND `edit-la-classe` | Manual |
| Après Minuit — After Dark | `after-dark` | Automated | Product tag equals `ready_to_sell` AND `edit-after-dark` | Manual |
| Les Romantiques | `les-romantiques` | Automated | Product tag equals `ready_to_sell` AND `edit-romantics` | Manual |
| La Famille | `la-famille` | Automated | Product tag equals `ready_to_sell` AND `edit-family` | Manual |

Use **all conditions** for automated collections. `ready_to_sell` means the product has approved photography, complete copy, pricing, variants, delivery information and is available to the Online Store channel.
