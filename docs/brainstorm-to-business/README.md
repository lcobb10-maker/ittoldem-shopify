# Brainstorm → Evidence → Business

An open, reusable workflow for turning ideas into responsible experiments, viable business decisions, and an accurate founder's journal.

## The first question is commercial
Before patent drafting or product design, ask: **Who pays, for what problem, and why now?** Record alternatives, willingness-to-pay evidence, expected costs, distribution, and reasons to stop.

## Workflow
1. Capture the idea verbatim; label each statement VERIFIED / REPORTED / PROPOSED / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN.
2. Assess customer, alternatives, market, pricing, unit economics, and commercial risks.
3. Search prior art, competitors, regulations, and safety constraints; link sources.
4. Design the smallest experiment that can disprove the central hypothesis.
5. Record observations, failures, measurements, photos and dates in a **private** evidence ledger.
6. Review IP with qualified counsel **before public disclosure** of enabling details.
7. Decide: proceed, pivot, license, pause, or stop.
8. Record the founder story: origin, experiments, collaborators, setbacks, decisions, and verified outcomes.

## Case study: fluid-delivery personal-care products (sanitized)
A founder reports an existing oil-dispensing comb and historical acrylic/laser experiments. Wearable and cosmetic extensions are proposed, not established. No private construction drawings, fluid-path dimensions, unreleased patent claims, or confidential formulation details are published here.

## Publication boundary
This repository contains **process templates only**. Do not upload confidential invention specifications, CAD, unpublished prototypes, personal information, private business numbers, or trade secrets. Public commits can be permanent. Filing and priority dates are not established by git history.

Educational resource, not legal advice.
